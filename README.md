# Bridging Classical and Data-Driven Transport Modelling

PhD viva presentation for the thesis *Bridging Classical and Data-Driven Transport Modelling: New Approaches to Traffic Prediction on the UK Road Network* (Yue Li, Department of Architecture, University of Cambridge). The deck follows the six thesis chapters, with backup slides at the end. A browser-based talk (reveal.js). **Live:** https://yueli901.github.io/classical-data-driven-transport-modelling/

- **Esc** or the grid button — map view (pan/zoom, click a slide) · **arrows** — navigate
- **S** speaker view · **C** pen · **Download PDF** — paper-style handout
- Works offline after first load. Built on [talk-html-template](https://github.com/yueli901/talk-html-template).
